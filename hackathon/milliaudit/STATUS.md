# MilliAudit status — 2026-08-15

## What ran

A 12-sample, CPU-only sweep completed on Modal using the real
`EvaMinkKinematicsSolver` and `model_x5.xml` from the pinned EgoVerse commit.
Each sample evaluated nominal IK plus 18 Cartesian perturbations:
`+/-x`, `+/-y`, and `+/-z` at 1, 2, and 3 mm.

The target path was deterministically generated by forward kinematics from a
joint sweep. It is **not** a recorded Eva or retargeted trajectory, so these
numbers are a solver/harness smoke result only.

| Perturbation | IK successes | Max residual | Max joint delta |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 mm | 72 / 72 | 0.0066 mm | 0.0142 rad |
| 2 mm | 72 / 72 | 0.00004 mm | 0.0286 rad |
| 3 mm | 72 / 72 | 0.00006 mm | 0.0431 rad |

Across all trials, the largest joint-limit margin reduction was 0.0126 rad.
The largest computed fragility score was 0.154 at 0.0 seconds; no IK failure or
large discontinuity appeared in this conservative generated sweep.

## Current blocker

The workspace has no recorded Eva end-effector target trajectory, rotation
sequence, or joint-state sequence. The public Mecka episodes used by EgoFlow
provide human video, not the Eva signals needed for IK retarget auditing.
Therefore MilliAudit has no empirical result yet. Supplying one trajectory in
the NPZ contract documented in `README.md` is the only missing input.

## Claim boundary

Even with a real trajectory, this experiment measures **kinematic fragility**.
IK sensitivity does not prove contact failure, task failure, or physical robot
failure without simulation/contact or hardware evidence.
